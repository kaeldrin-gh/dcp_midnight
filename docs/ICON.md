# Forever icon

Adapted from the owner's supplied Midnight icon with the built-in image generation tool. The skull and circular rune frame are retained; the new palette uses bronze/gold, azure, turquoise, and deep navy, inspired by the [official Forever branding](https://gear.blizzard.com/collections/world-of-warcraft-forever-merchandise).

`Media/Icon.png` is the full-resolution generated artwork for repository and listing use. `Media/Icon.tga` is a 256 × 256, uncompressed 24-bit RGB export for WoW's AddOns list, referenced by `## IconTexture` in the TOC. This branding update does not change the ability icons used for cooldown pulses.

The final prompt used with the built-in tool was:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: square World of Warcraft addon icon. Image 1 is the EDIT TARGET: user's original Doom CooldownPulse Midnight skull icon. Image 2 is only a COLOR REFERENCE: official Forever branded shirt, use the sand-gold and sea-blue palette from its printed emblem; do NOT include the shirt, its fabric, its W logo, or any letters in the final icon. Adapt Image 1 to a WoW Forever-inspired palette. Preserve the exact central menacing elongated horned skull silhouette, its position, the circular carved rune ring, and the overall radial pulse composition. Change the steel-gray ring to aged warm bronze with gold edges and restrained azure rune glows. Replace violet/purple cosmic lightning with luminous azure, turquoise, and sea-blue magical energy, interspersed with warm sun-gold pulse light. Make the skull dark bronze/obsidian with warm gold highlights and cyan-blue eyes so it remains readable at 32px. Background deep ocean navy, not purple. Painterly high-detail fantasy game inventory icon, substantial contrast and clear center, symmetric centered composition. Square, opaque full-bleed artwork filling the entire canvas, keep the ring fully inside the edges. No readable text, no letters, no game logo, no watermark. The final image must contain only the icon.
